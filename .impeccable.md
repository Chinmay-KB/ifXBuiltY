## Design Context

### Users
People who want to create and share a funny-but-plausible parody “product screenshot from an alternate timeline” in under 30 seconds. The primary context is social posting (X/Twitter, IG, LinkedIn), so the UI must bias toward fast comprehension, remixability, and clean share surfaces.

### Brand Personality
Playful, sharp, internet-native. Confident editorial typography with a “lab/specimen” vibe, but not sci‑fi. Humor is in the details, not gimmicks.

### Aesthetic Direction
High-contrast light mode, bold headline typography (Archivo Black), clean neutrals, a single loud accent (highlighter yellow). Layouts should feel like an art-directed magazine spread: asymmetric, crisp alignment, lots of breathing room, strong hierarchy.

### Design Principles
- The joke is the product — the UI should get out of the way and spotlight the image + prompt.
- One strong accent beats many “pretty” accents.
- Alignment is non-negotiable: establish lanes and stick to them.
- Spacing rhythm: tight within groups, generous between groups.
- Share/remix actions should be obvious, fast, and never visually noisy.
